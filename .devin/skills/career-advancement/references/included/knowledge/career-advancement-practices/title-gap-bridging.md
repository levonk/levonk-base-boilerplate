# Title Gap Bridging

## The Title Gap Problem

Recruiters scan resumes looking for recognizable signals. If your current
title doesn't match the target role, the recruiter may not recognize you as a
fit — even if your experience is perfect. The title gap creates a moment of
confusion, and in a 7.4-second scan, confusion means rejection.

## Bridging Strategies

### 1. Parenthetical Clarifiers

Add a parenthetical explanation to your title that translates it into the
target role's language:

| Your title | Target role | Bridged title |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| Associate | Senior Analyst | Associate (Functionally Senior Analyst) |
| Staff Engineer | Engineering Manager | Staff Engineer (Functionally leading 12-engineer team) |
| Product Manager | Program Manager | Product Manager (Functionally leading roadmap) |
| Tech Lead | VP Engineering | Tech Lead (Functionally VP of Engineering for 40-person org) |

The parenthetical should be:
- **Short** — 3–7 words
- **Functional** — Describes what you actually do, not what you wish you were
- **Honest** — Don't overstate; the interview will reveal the truth

### 2. Dot-Connector Summary

If your experience is perfect but your title doesn't translate, lead with a
one-line summary that connects the dots:

> Senior Engineer with 8 years of functionally leading cross-functional
> teams, now targeting Engineering Management roles.

This works well in the summary section at the top of the resume. It tells the
recruiter: "I know my title doesn't say 'Manager,' but here's why I am one."

### 3. Functional Title in the Summary

If your official title is misleading, use a functional title in your summary
and explain the official title in the experience section:

**Summary:**
> Engineering Leader with 10+ years building and scaling distributed systems
> teams...

**Experience:**
> **Staff Software Engineer** (Functionally Engineering Lead), Google —
> 2020–2024

## When to Use Each Strategy

| Situation | Strategy |
|-----------|---------|
| Title is close but not exact | Parenthetical clarifier |
| Title is in a different domain | Dot-connector summary |
| Title is misleading or internal-only | Functional title in summary + explanation in experience |
| Title is junior but scope is senior | Parenthetical with scope ("Associate (Functionally leading senior work)") |

## What Not to Do

- **Don't change your official title** — Use the real title and add a
  clarifier. Changing the title outright is dishonest and will be discovered
  in background checks.
- **Don't over-explain** — One parenthetical or one summary line is enough.
  A paragraph of explanation signals defensiveness.
- **Don't apologize for the gap** — State the functional reality confidently.
  The title gap is a translation problem, not a qualification problem.
