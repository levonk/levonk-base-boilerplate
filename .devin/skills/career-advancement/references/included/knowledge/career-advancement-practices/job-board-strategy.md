# Job Board Strategy

## Job Boards Are the 10% Bucket

Job boards are the **lowest-signal channel** in the job search. They are
useful for discovery and market scanning, but they should consume only about
10% of your search time (see [Job Search Strategy](job-search-strategy.md)
— the 70-20-10 rule). The mistake is treating them as the primary channel.

### Why Job Boards Are Low Signal

- **High competition.** The same posting is visible to thousands.
- **Low context.** You are a resume in a pile, not a referred person.
- **Generic descriptions.** Job postings are often templated and don't
  reflect the actual team or work.

## The Board Landscape

| Board | Strengths | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **LinkedIn** | Default board; maximum visibility; recruiter inbound | Broad visibility, mid-to-senior roles |
| **Indeed** | Highest volume of postings | Volume scanning, non-tech and mixed roles |
| **AngelList / Wellfound** | Direct founder contact, equity transparency | Startups (seed to Series B) |
| **Hired.com** | Companies apply to you; profile-based | Mid-to-senior tech roles |
| **Dice** | Tech-specific, deep filter options | Tech-specific roles, contract work |
| **BuiltIn** | Curated startup hubs (BuiltIn NYC, SF, Austin) | Startup ecosystem roles by city |
| **Company career pages** | Highest signal — direct application, no middleman | Target companies you've already identified |

## When to Use Each

### LinkedIn

The **default board** — good for visibility and recruiter inbound, but high
competition means conversion is mediocre. Best used alongside a strong
profile (see [LinkedIn Profile
Optimization](linkedin-profile-optimization.md)) so recruiters find you
rather than (or in addition to) you applying cold.

### Indeed

**Volume over quality.** Useful for casting a wide net in industries or
regions where LinkedIn is less dominant. Expect more noise — lower-quality
postings, more scam-adjacent listings. Filter aggressively.

### Specialized Boards

| If You Are... | Use... | Because... |
|---------------|--------|-----------|
| Targeting startups | AngelList / Wellfound | Direct line to founders, equity details upfront |
| A senior engineer | Hired.com | Companies come to you; less pile competition |
| Tech-specific (contract) | Dice | Deep tech filters, contract-friendly |
| In a startup hub | BuiltIn | Curated, local, less noise than LinkedIn |

### Company Career Pages

**Highest signal of any channel.** Applying directly on the company's
career page means you're in their ATS with no intermediary. This is
especially true for companies that don't post widely.

| Advantage | Why It Matters |
|-----------|----------------|
| No middleman | Your application goes straight to the employer's ATS |
| Shows intent | Applying on the career page signals genuine interest |
| Often the only channel | Many companies don't cross-post to boards |

## The "Easy Apply" Trap

**Easy Apply** (one-click applications on LinkedIn and similar) feels
efficient — but **low effort in equals low signal out**.

| Easy Apply | Standard Application |
|-----------|---------------------|
| One click, same resume everywhere | Tailored resume per role |
| No cover letter | Personalized cover letter |
| Feels productive | Is productive |
| Low conversion | Higher conversion |

### When Easy Apply Is Acceptable

- **Discovery and pipeline-filling.** Use it to quickly get a posting into
  your tracker for follow-up via a referral.
- **When you have a referral already.** The referral carries the signal;
  the easy apply is just the mechanism.

### When It Is Not

- As your **primary application method.** If 90% of your applications are
  one-click, you are in the inverted-ratio trap (see [Job Search
  Strategy](job-search-strategy.md)).

## The "Referral First" Principle

**Before you apply cold to any posting, try to find a connection.**

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| You know someone at the company | Ask for a referral (see [Networking and Relationship Building](networking-relationship-building.md) — How to Ask for a Referral) |
| You have a dormant tie there | Reactivate the connection first, then apply |
| No connection at all | Apply, but also reach out to a relevant person on LinkedIn with context |

### Why Referral First

A referral can **bypass the ATS pile entirely** — your resume goes straight
to the hiring manager. Conversion rates for referred candidates are
dramatically higher than cold applications (see [ATS Reality vs
Myths](ats-reality-vs-myths.md)).

### The Workflow

1. **Find the posting** (on a board or company page).
2. **Search your network** — LinkedIn, alumni database, former colleagues.
3. **If a connection exists** — warm it, then ask for a referral.
4. **If no connection** — apply directly on the career page, and separately
   do cold outreach to someone on the team.

## Job Alerts and Saved Searches

Set up **saved searches and alerts** so the postings come to you rather than
requiring daily manual scanning.

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Search radius | Tight enough to be relevant, wide enough to catch remote |
| Keywords | Use the same keywords you'd target in a resume (see [Keyword Engineering](keyword-engineering.md)) |
| Alert frequency | Daily — weekly is too slow for early application advantage |
| Seniority filter | Set it — don't get junior-level noise if you're senior |

### Why Alerts Matter

They **reduce active scanning time** — the 10% bucket stays small because
you're not manually browsing. You only engage when a relevant posting lands
in your inbox.

## The Timing of Applications

**When you apply matters** as much as where.

| Timing | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **First 48 hours after posting** | Highest conversion — you're in the initial pile |
| **Tuesday–Wednesday** | Best days — recruiters review fresh postings midweek |
| **Weekends** | Avoid — your application sits until Monday, buried by then |
| **Late in the posting window** | Low conversion — the pile is already large |

### Why Early Wins

Recruiters often review applications in **batches** as they arrive. The
first batch is small and gets careful attention. By day 5, the pile is
hundreds deep and screening becomes ruthless (see [ATS Reality vs
Myths](ats-reality-vs-myths.md) — Apply Early).

### Practical Workflow

1. Alerts surface a relevant posting.
2. Within 24 hours: check for a referral connection.
3. If referral exists: send tailored resume + ask for referral.
4. If no referral: apply directly on the career page with a tailored resume
   and personalized cover letter (see [Application
   Strategy](application-strategy.md)).
