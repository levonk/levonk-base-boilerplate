# Proving Ground Principle

## The Principle

A resume is a signal of your ability to solve **today's** problems, not a
comprehensive archive of your life. The last **5–7 years** are your proving
ground — the period that most credibly demonstrates your current capability.

Experience older than 7 years is not irrelevant, but it should be weighted
differently: summarized, not detailed.

## Weighting by Relevance

Do not give the same space to a job from 2004 as you do to a job from 2024.
The recent roles should occupy **70–80%** of the resume's space.

| Role recency | Space allocation | Detail level |
|-------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Current role | 25–30% of resume | Full detail: 4–5 bullets, metrics, scope |
| 1–3 years ago | 20–25% of resume | Full detail: 3–4 bullets, metrics |
| 3–7 years ago | 15–20% of resume | Moderate detail: 2–3 bullets, key achievements |
| 7–15 years ago | 5–10% of resume | Summary: 1–2 bullets or a single line |
| 15+ years ago | 0–5% of resume | Omit entirely, or include in a condensed "Additional Experience" paragraph |

## Trimming Older Roles

### What to Keep

For roles older than 7 years, keep only:
- **Credibility anchors** — Company names that are recognizable (e.g., "Google,"
  "Amazon") or scale markers (e.g., "$700M+/yr revenue")
- **Award signals** — Major industry or company-wide awards
- **Founding/early-stage markers** — Employee numbers, founding team roles

### What to Cut

- Day-to-day responsibilities (they are assumed and not relevant)
- Technologies that are now legacy (they signal age, not capability)
- Metrics that are no longer impressive in the current market
- Internal company context that no longer translates

### The Condensed Format

For pre-2012 roles, use a single narrative paragraph rather than
role-by-role bullets. This is the "Additional Experience" pattern:

> **Additional Experience:** Engineering leadership roles at Yahoo! (employee
> #1, $700M+/yr platform), UberMedia/Idealab ($29.6M VC), and Vivendi
> Universal. Built and scaled distributed systems handling 18,000 QPS at peak.

This preserves the credibility signals (company names, scale metrics) without
the age-signaling detail of dates, titles, and bullet-point descriptions.

## The Relevance Filter

For every bullet on the resume, ask:

> "Does this reinforce what I am doing now for the specific job I am targeting?"

If the answer is no, the bullet is noise. Remove it. A resume that is 100%
relevant is more powerful than a resume that is 100% comprehensive.

## Common Mistakes

- **Equal weighting** — Giving a 2010 role the same space as a 2024 role.
  This signals that you haven't grown.
- **Legacy technology** — Listing technologies from 15+ years ago. This
  signals that you haven't kept current. Move legacy tech to an archive
  document, not the modern resume.
- **Nostalgia bullets** — "Built the company's first website in 1998." This
  is interesting history but not a current capability signal.
- **Fear of omission** — Worrying that removing old roles will create gaps.
  Condensed "Additional Experience" entries prevent gaps without requiring
  full detail.
