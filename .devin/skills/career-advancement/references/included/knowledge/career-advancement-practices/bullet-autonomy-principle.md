# Bullet Autonomy Principle

## The Core Principle

**Every bullet must stand alone.** A recruiter scanning a resume reads bullets
out of order, in isolation, and at speed (see [Visual Scanning
Patterns](visual-scanning-patterns.md) — the 7.4-second scan). If a bullet
depends on the bullet above it for context, it fails when read in isolation.

A self-contained bullet has three elements:

1. **Action** — what you did (see [Ownership Verbs](ownership-verbs.md))
2. **Object** — what you did it to or for
3. **Outcome** — what changed because of the action (see [Metrics and
   Quantification](metrics-and-quantification.md))

## The Sub-Point Trap

The most common autonomy failure is the **sub-point bullet** — a bullet that
is actually a detail of the bullet above it, promoted to look like an
independent achievement.

**Fails autonomy:**

> * Championed in-house marketing media mix and analytics platform.
> * Engaged senior leaders across business segments to drive adoption.
> * Supported Parks & Resorts in evaluating Studio platforms.

The second and third bullets are sub-points of the first. Read in isolation,
"Engaged senior leaders across business segments" means nothing — engaged them
to do what? "Supported Parks & Resorts in evaluating Studio platforms" is a
detail of the broader analytics platform adoption, not a standalone
achievement.

**Passes autonomy (merged):**

> * Championed in-house marketing media mix and analytics platform — engaged
>   senior leaders across 5 business segments to drive adoption, including
>   guiding Parks & Resorts in evaluating Studio platforms.

The sub-points become supporting detail within the single autonomous bullet.

## The Action-Object-Outcome Test

Read each bullet and ask:

1. **What did I do?** (Action — is there an ownership verb?)
2. **What did I do it to or for?** (Object — is the target clear?)
3. **What changed?** (Outcome — is there a measurable or describable result?)

If any element is missing or depends on another bullet for context, the bullet
fails the test.

| Fails | Passes |
|-------|--------|
| `Engaged senior leaders across business segments` | `Engaged senior leaders across 5 business segments to drive adoption of the analytics platform, enabling $1B in attributed revenue` |
| `Supported Parks & Resorts` | `Guided Parks & Resorts in evaluating Studio platforms for cross-segment analytics adoption` |
| `Led weekly syncs` | `Aligned roadmap priorities across 5 product teams, reducing cross-team dependencies by 40%` |

## When to Merge vs. Split

### Merge when:

- The bullet is a sub-point of another bullet (it elaborates on the same
  achievement)
- The bullet can't stand alone without the bullet above it
- The bullet adds detail but not a distinct achievement

### Split when:

- The bullet describes a genuinely distinct achievement (different project,
  different outcome, different scope)
- The bullet has its own action, object, and outcome
- The bullet would be too long if merged (see [Conciseness and
  Length](conciseness-and-length.md))

## The Scannability Connection

Recruiters scan in an F-shape, reading the left third and the first few bullets
of each role (see [Visual Scanning Patterns](visual-scanning-patterns.md)).
Sub-point bullets that are promoted to top-level bullets steal scan attention
from the actual achievements. Every sub-point bullet pushes a real achievement
further down the scan path.

## Relationship to Other Concepts

- **[Ownership Verbs](ownership-verbs.md)** — The action element of an
  autonomous bullet must lead with an ownership verb.
- **[Metrics and Quantification](metrics-and-quantification.md)** — The
  outcome element should be quantified where possible.
- **[Conciseness and Length](conciseness-and-length.md)** — Merging
  sub-points into their parent bullet keeps the bullet count within the sweet
  spot.
- **[Visual Scanning Patterns](visual-scanning-patterns.md)** — Autonomous
  bullets survive the out-of-order scan; sub-point bullets don't.
- **[Safe Pair of Hands Positioning](safe-pair-of-hands-positioning.md)** —
  Each autonomous bullet should communicate "done it before" credibility on
  its own.
